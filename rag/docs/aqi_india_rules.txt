Air Quality Index (AQI) is a tool to communicate air quality in simple and understandable terms.

AQI converts complex air pollution data into a single number, category, and description.

There are six AQI categories:
Good,
Satisfactory,
Moderately Polluted,
Poor,
Very Poor,
Severe.

AQI is calculated using eight pollutants:
PM10, PM2.5, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, and Pb.

The worst sub-index among all pollutants determines the overall AQI value.
